Underlying performancePer-minute / per-opportunity rates, shot quality, targets, xG, pitch mix.

What to compare before calling a bet +EV

  • Shop the same market across multiple books and exchanges.
  • Remove the bookmaker margin before comparing probabilities.
  • Compare your fair price with the best executable price, not an old screenshot.
  • Track closing-line value to test whether your prices beat the market close.
  • Record actual results over a large sample with the real odds and pushes handled correctly.

Handicapper and expert-pick audit

Elite Wagers does not label personalities “sharp” because they are famous, loud or popular. Evaluate published records like a performance dataset.

CAPTRACKER's 2026 published audit reported a −0.58% aggregate return across 6,906 graded picks from 1,384 handicappers (January–August 2026). This is one dataset with its own collection scope, not a universal estimate of all bettors. It illustrates why verified records matter. Read its methodology and report.

Minimum standard for evaluating a bettor

Timestamped picksCaptured before the event, including deleted or edited picks where possible.
Real odds and stakesGrade at posted prices; include units, juice, pushes and voids.
Closing-line valueCompare the bet's price with the market close on the same market.
Sample size and splitsSeparate sport, league, market, price band and time period.
Full recordInclude losses and all picks, not only winning streaks or selected screenshots.
Out-of-sample consistencyCheck whether performance persists after the original sample and across seasons.
