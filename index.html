What to compare before calling a bet +EV

  • Shop the same market across multiple books and exchanges.
  • Remove the bookmaker margin before comparing fair probability.
  • Track how your model’s estimated probability performs after the fact.
  • Label edge as a research signal, not as guaranteed profit.

Handicapper and expert-pick audit

Elite Wagers does not label personalities “sharp” because they are famous, loud or popular. Evaluate published records with objective filters.

CAPTRACKER's 2026 published audit reported a −0.58% aggregate return across 6,906 graded picks from 1,384 handicappers (January–August 2026). This is one dataset with its own methodology and limits.

Minimum standard for evaluating a bettor

Timestamped picksCaptured before the event, including deleted or edited picks where possible.
Real odds and stakesUse actual closing odds, stake information, and voids or pushes to grade correctly.
Sample size and CLVLook for meaningful sample size, ROI stability, and vetted closing-line value before trusting a trend.
